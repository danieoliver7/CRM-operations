# Operational Planning

## Planning Philosophy

The system should help users:
- anticipate overload
- visualize future operations
- understand operational pressure
- coordinate campaigns
- distribute work

## Operational Planning Goals

- overload perception
- squad coordination
- operational calendar visibility
- owner visibility
- campaign distribution

## Capacity Indicators

Examples:
- too many campaigns scheduled
- overloaded QA
- overloaded CRM operator
- urgent campaign concentration

## Operational Pressure

Operational pressure is a lightweight perception layer derived from campaigns.

It can highlight:
- overloaded days
- overloaded owners
- overloaded squads
- overloaded workflow stages
- urgent campaign concentration

It should describe what is happening in the plan, not pretend to predict the future.

Correct:
- 5 campaigns planned for Friday
- Daniel has 3 urgent campaigns
- QA has high campaign concentration

Avoid:
- AI prediction language
- automatic planning recommendations
- optimization claims
- hidden scoring systems

## Important

Planning should remain:
- lightweight
- visual
- operational

Avoid:
- scheduling engines
- AI forecasting
- automatic optimization
- complex planning systems
