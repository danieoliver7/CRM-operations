# Capacity Philosophy

Capacity indicators exist to:
- expose overload
- visualize operational pressure
- help campaign coordination

## Capacity Is NOT

- AI prediction
- automatic balancing
- resource optimization
- workforce management software

## Current Capacity Indicators

- campaigns per day
- campaigns per owner
- campaigns per squad
- urgent campaign concentration
- overloaded workflow stages

## Warning Hierarchy

Capacity warnings should use a simple hierarchy:
- normal: no relevant pressure signal
- watch: elevated concentration that deserves attention
- overloaded: clear operational concentration that may affect delivery

## Overload Heuristics

Heuristics should remain explicit and easy to understand:
- count active campaigns by due date
- count active campaigns by owner
- count active campaigns by squad
- count active campaigns by workflow stage
- increase attention when urgent campaigns are concentrated

These heuristics are product signals, not scheduling rules.

## Operational Pressure Principles

Capacity indicators should:
- be derived from current campaign data
- explain the pressure in plain operational language
- help teams prioritize review
- stay visually subtle unless pressure is high

Capacity indicators should NOT:
- rebalance work automatically
- assign owners automatically
- forecast with AI
- become a planning engine

## UX Philosophy

Warnings should:
- guide
- inform
- prioritize

Warnings should NOT:
- overwhelm
- pollute
- generate unnecessary fear
