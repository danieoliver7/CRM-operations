# SLA Philosophy

SLA exists to expose operational timing expectations.

The goal is visibility, not enforcement.

---

# Examples

- overdue copy
- overdue QA
- overdue approval
- due soon
- delayed workflow stage

---

# Important

SLA indicators should:
- remain lightweight
- remain visual
- avoid enterprise complexity

Avoid:
- timers everywhere
- SLA engines
- escalation runtimes
- automation workflows

---

# Current SLA Indicators

SLA awareness should use explicit states:
- on-track
- due soon
- delayed
- overdue

# SLA Heuristics

Current frontend heuristics:
- due today or tomorrow: due soon
- active campaign past due date: delayed or overdue
- sent and completed campaigns: on track

SLA indicators should describe timing pressure, not enforce workflow rules.
