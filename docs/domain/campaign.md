# Campaign Domain

## Campaign Philosophy
Campaign is the central operational entity of the platform.

Every operational flow revolves around Campaign:
- planning
- production
- QA
- scheduling
- delivery
- coordination

## Campaign Core Fields

- id
- name
- channel
- priority
- owner
- squad
- status
- dueDate
- objective
- audience
- tags

## Campaign Statuses

- briefing
- copy
- approval
- development
- qa
- scheduled
- sent
- completed

## Campaign Channels

- email
- sms
- push
- whatsapp
- inapp
- webpush

## Campaign Priorities

- low
- medium
- high
- urgent

## Campaign Philosophy

Campaigns should:
- communicate operational state
- expose workflow context
- guide operational action
- synchronize operational visibility

Campaigns should NOT:
- behave like generic cards
- be only visual entities
- exist disconnected from workflow

## Operational Context

Every campaign stage must provide:
- contextual actions
- contextual risks
- next actions
- workflow guidance
